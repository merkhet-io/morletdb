# Why C2 missed machine M03's anomalies (exploratory, after the result)

This analysis was done after C2's single evaluation, on its already-consumed data. It
explains the result; it is not evidence, and any fix needs a fresh test.

## Finding: drift in impulsiveness values hides the anomalies

- **Drift on M03's y axis.** Normal M03 runs in 2021 differ from their 2019–2020
  same-operation history:
  - y-axis kurtosis is up 72% (median signed relative deviation);
  - y-axis crest factor is up 40%;
  - vibration levels (std) and duration are unchanged.

  M01 and M02 show at most 37% on one value (M02, z-axis kurtosis), mostly under
  10%.
- **Normal runs score high.** The median score of a normal 2021 run is 0.76 on M03,
  against 0.26 on M01 and 0.41 on M02.
- **The anomalies rank low.** Within their own operation, among their normal 2021
  peers, 4 of M03's 6 anomalous runs rank in the lower half, one sits at the
  median and one ranks at the top (OP02).

## Variants (post hoc, same data; AUROC bad vs good)

| Variant | All | M01 | M02 | M03 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Frozen: max over 10 values, all same-context history | 0.81 | 0.96 | 0.92 | 0.39 |
| Latest history period only | 0.71 | 0.94 | 0.63 | 0.30 |
| Duration only | 0.92 | 0.97 | 0.74 | 0.93 |
| Duration + std per axis | 0.92 | 0.96 | 0.99 | 0.95 |

- **Recent history is not the fix.** It shrinks history without removing the
  drift.
- **The anomalies are visible in duration and vibration level.** The maximum over
  values let one drifting value dominate.

## Design consequences (hypotheses for phase 2, to be tested on fresh data)

1. **Do not combine values by an unweighted maximum.** Scale each value's
   deviation by how much that value normally varies in its context and period, or
   report values separately.
2. **Make duration first-class.** It was the strongest single value in C2.
3. **Show per-value deviations,** so drift (many normal runs high on one value) is
   visible as drift instead of becoming alarms.

Selecting "duration + std" here would be tuning on consumed evaluation data. It is
recorded as a hypothesis, not adopted as a result.
