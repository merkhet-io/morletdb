Notes from the build

Design ideas, results and open questions from building MorletDB in public. Plans here are proposals until an issue, a test and a result say otherwise.

  • 8 November 2026 · Design idea and first result
    A fast model decides, Claude explains

    A System 1 model (TypeSafe's Jev) decided from MorletDB's decision state alone and ranked worn milling inserts nearly as well as the purpose-built score (AUROC 0.948 against 0.966); Claude then explained one of its decisions from the same recordings and found a weakness in the state.

  • 8 November 2026 · Paper
    Report 1 · Searching waveforms by shape inside PostgreSQL

    Ordinary vector search misses shifted matches; 13 shifted probes into one standard PostgreSQL index found 97.7% of the exact top ten on seismic data, every hit traceable to stored samples.

  • 8 November 2026 · Paper
    Report 2 · An archive that forgets on purpose

    A draft report on machine archives as episodic memory with graded, recorded forgetting; a first study shows exempting known events keeps them all findable, but retention by age stays the default.

  • 8 November 2026 · Paper
    Report 3 · Sending a tenth of the bytes and keeping faults visible

    A plain wavelet coder at a tenth of lossless size beat SZ3 and ZFP and ran in real time on a Raspberry Pi, but seismic and screwdriving diagnosis did not fully survive.

  • 7 November 2026 · Design idea · updated 8 November
    Self-driving Postgres on the edge

    Taking the self-driving Postgres idea of Postgres AI to the shop floor: why MorletDB should maintain itself on the machine, and where it must stop and ask. Updated with the first results: a disk filled on purpose on a Raspberry Pi, new recordings refused before it was full, nothing lost.
