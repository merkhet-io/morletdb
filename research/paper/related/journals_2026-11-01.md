# Journal requirements check: MorletDB manuscript

Retrieval date for every source below: **2026-11-01**. Sources are official journal or publisher pages only, read via WebFetch or a read-only browser tab. I did not log in, create an account, contact anyone or submit anything. I recorded no acceptance rates or review times, because no official page I read gave them as a commitment.

Manuscript baseline: about 10 pages in ACM format plus a 4-page supplement. It is a research paper: a lag-tolerant waveform retrieval method, a PostgreSQL/Rust integration, and an evaluation. The code is Apache-2.0 at github.com/merkhet-io/morletdb. The repository was created 2026-10-25 and the first commit was 2026-10-23. Sole author, no affiliation, no funding.

### JORS
- https://openresearchsoftware.metajnl.com/about/submissions
  - Metapapers "do not contain research results but rather a concise description of research software"
  - Software must be "deposited in a data repository under an open licence (such as an OSI approved license…)"
  - Checklist: "The code is a substantive intellectual contribution"
  - Checklist: "easy to install and supports versioning" (registry such as "crates.io (Rust)")
  - Five potential reviewers and a cover letter are required
  - APC: "Software Metapapers £824.00", "Issues in Research Software £891.00", tax extra
  - Waiver: "please outline this in your cover letter"; "Editorial decisions are made independently from the ability to pay the APC."
  - Issues in Research Software: "no longer than 3000 - 4000 words"
  - Authors keep copyright; articles are CC BY.
- https://openresearchsoftware.metajnl.com/about
  - Scope: "Software Metapapers describing research software with high reuse potential"
  - Also "full-length research papers" on creating, maintaining and evaluating research software (the Issues section)
  - "This journal publishes continuously"
  - Recommended repositories: "We require that the version of software described … is available in at least one repository"; ideally both types; a preservation repository should provide "a unique, persistent identifier (e.g. a DOI)". Zenodo, figshare and GitHub are among those listed.
- https://openresearchsoftware.metajnl.com/about/editorialpolicies
  - "Peer review is typically single anonymous"
  - Reviewers "download the software" and check it "behaves as described"

### JOSS
- https://joss.readthedocs.io/en/latest/submitting.html
  - Scope and significance: "at least six months of public history prior to submission, with evidence of releases, public issues and pull requests"
  - Gate 1: "The repository must have been public for more than six months prior to submission, with active development spanning that period."
  - Also: "development concentrated into a few days or weeks, will not be accepted" and "a repo dump is not a history"
  - Gate 2: evidence the software is used for research; "Aspirational statements about future use are not sufficient"
  - Gate 3: single-author projects need several signals (commit history, releases or changelog, tests/CI, docs, CONTRIBUTING, support statement)
  - Gate 4: ongoing iteration, not a single burst
  - Co-publication: "the implementation of the software itself reflects a substantial scientific effort"
  - On rejection: "resubmit in six months or more"
  - **Our repo:** public since 2026-10-25 (first commit 2026-10-23), so about one week of history on 2026-11-01. Fails gate 1, and almost certainly gates 2 and 4. Earliest eligibility on gate 1 is after about 2027-04-25, and it requires continuous development until then.
